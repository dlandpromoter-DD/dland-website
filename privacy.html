Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

D Land Promoters & Developers respects the privacy of people who contact us through this website. This page explains how information submitted through the website is handled.

Information you provide

If you complete the enquiry form, the website prepares a message containing the details you enter and opens WhatsApp so you can choose to send it to our team. The static website itself does not save your form details to a database.

Third-party services

WhatsApp, Google Fonts, Google Maps and your internet service may process information according to their own privacy policies when you use their services or follow links from this website.

How we use enquiries

If you send us an enquiry, we may use the information you provide to respond to your property questions, discuss project availability or coordinate a site visit. We do not promise that any property is available until our team confirms it.

Your choices

You can choose not to submit an enquiry. Please avoid sending sensitive personal or financial information through a general enquiry form or chat.

Contact
